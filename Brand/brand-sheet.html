Three bars, heavier — the small-size cut

Below roughly 24px on screen, or 10mm in print, switch to the three-bar cut. The top bar in the four-bar mark is 8 units wide in a 100-unit box — at 16px that's 1.3px, and it either disappears or merges into its neighbour depending on the printer. The three-bar version drops the weakest tone (the soft brown), thickens what's left and keeps the ink→blue→terracotta signature, so the mark stays recognisably the same object at a tenth the size.

Keep clear space of x on every side, where x is the thickness of the bottom terracotta bar. Nothing — no rule, no photo edge, no other logo — comes inside it. It scales with the mark, so there's one number to remember instead of a table.

On the crew

Small, one colour, and competing with hi-vis. The three-bar cut earns its keep here.

Hard hat — three-bar, 30mm

SAKOKHULE
TRADING (PTY) LTD

Hi-vis chest — one colour, ink

On hi-vis the mark goes one-colour ink, never full colour: terracotta on lime is a genuinely bad pairing, and PPE is printed or embroidered in as few threads as possible anyway. Ink on hi-vis lime measures well past 4.5:1, so it stays legible at the distance a flagman is actually seen from.
